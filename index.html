One list. Everyone who shops.

Grocery List keeps your shopping lists on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. Share a list with family or housemates, and everyone can add items and check them off as they shop.

Lists you share

Keep as many lists as you need: groceries, the hardware store, a party. Invite people with their own Apple Account to a list, and changes show up for everyone through iCloud.

Say it instead of typing it

Dictate what you need, in English or Ukrainian, and the app turns it into items. You check them before they're added.

A photo is worth a brand name

Attach photos to a list or to a single item, so whoever goes to the shop picks up exactly the right thing.

Your data stays yours

  • No account, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Lists and photos live on your device and in your own iCloud. The developer runs no server and receives nothing.
  • A list is shared only with the people you invite. Privacy policy.
