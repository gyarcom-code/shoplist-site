Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

The developer of Shared Grocery & Shopping List collects nothing and runs no server. There is no account and no sign-up. Your lists and photos are stored on your device and in your own iCloud, and are shared only with the people you invite.

Where your lists and photos are stored

Your lists, their items and the photos you attach are stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on for the app, they are also stored in your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit, so they stay in sync across your devices. That storage is provided by Apple and is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy; the developer cannot access it.

Sharing a list

A list is shared only when you share it, and only with the people you invite. Sharing uses Apple's iCloud sharing: the list stays in the iCloud account of the person who created it, and the people you invite can see and change that list, including its items and photos. You can stop sharing, or remove someone, at any time. Lists you don't share stay private to you.

Microphone and speech recognition

The app asks for access to the microphone and to speech recognition only so you can dictate items. It listens only while you are dictating. The audio is processed by Apple's speech recognition: on your device where your device and language support it, otherwise by Apple. The app does not keep the recording; only the items you confirm are saved to your list.

Photos and the camera

You can attach photos to a list or to an item, for example a photo of a shelf, a recipe or the inside of your fridge. Photos you add are stored on your device. Once list sharing is available, they will also be stored in your iCloud with the list, and people you share that list with will be able to see them.

When you add a photo, the app makes a smaller copy and saves only that. Location and all other metadata (such as the date and time, camera model and settings) are removed from the copy; the original in your photo library is not changed.

When you choose a photo from your library, the app receives only the photos you select; it does not get access to the rest of your library. The app asks for access to the camera only so you can take photos for your lists, and photos you take this way are not saved to your library. The app saves a photo to your library only if you choose "Save Image" when sharing a photo.

What the app never does

  • No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting services.
  • No advertising, no advertising identifiers, no tracking of any kind.
  • No third-party SDKs.
  • No account, no email address, no password, no profile.
  • No developer server. The developer receives none of your data.

Deleting your data

Deleting a list in the app deletes it from your device and from your iCloud. Deleting the app removes the data on your device; the copy in your iCloud can be removed in your device's iCloud settings. Like other app data, it can also be included in your device's own iCloud or computer backup.

Children

Shared Grocery & Shopping List is a general-purpose tool. It is not directed at children and the developer collects no information from anyone.

Changes to this policy
