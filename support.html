Do I need an account?

No. The app uses the iCloud account you're already signed in to on your iPhone. To sync and share lists, iCloud needs to be turned on for the app in the Settings app under your name › iCloud.

How do I share a list?

Open the list and use Share to invite people. Each person needs their own Apple Account and iCloud. Once they accept, everyone on the list can add items and check them off, and changes reach the others through iCloud.

Changes don't show up on another iPhone

  • Check that both iPhones are online and signed in to iCloud, with iCloud turned on for the app.
  • Check that iCloud storage isn't full on the account that owns the list.
  • Sync usually takes a few seconds, sometimes longer on a slow connection.

Voice input doesn't understand me

Dictation currently supports English and Ukrainian. Allow microphone and speech recognition access when the app asks; you can change this later in the Settings app under Privacy & Security › Microphone and › Speech Recognition. Short phrases separated by pauses or "and" work best, and you can correct items before they're added.
